Fahad Rahman Amik is a PhD Candidate in Electrical & Computer Engineering at Mila - Quebec AI Institute and McGill University, supervised by [Prof. Boris Vaisband](https://think-team.github.io/team/members/boris-vaisband/) and [Prof. Warren Gross](https://www.mcgill.ca/ece/warren-gross). His research focuses on Machine Learning for Electronic Design Automation (ML for EDA), with particular interests in Graph Representation Learning, Timing Analysis, and Physical Design.
